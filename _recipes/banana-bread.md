---
title: Banana bread
author: Sam
lang: en
tags: [dessert, baking]
ingredients:
  - 3 ripe bananas
  - 75 g butter, melted
  - 150 g sugar
  - 1 egg, beaten
  - 1 tsp vanilla extract
  - 1 tsp baking soda
  - A pinch of salt
  - 190 g plain flour
---

1. Preheat the oven to 175 C and butter a loaf pan.
2. Mash the bananas in a large bowl with a fork.
3. Stir in the melted butter, then the sugar, egg and vanilla.
4. Sprinkle the baking soda and salt over the mixture and mix well.
5. Add the flour and stir until just combined.
6. Pour into the pan and bake for about 60 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.
7. Let it cool in the pan for 10 minutes before slicing.
