---
title: Lentil soup
author: Eleni
tags: [soup, vegan]
translated_from: el
ingredients:
  - 500 g lentils
  - 1 onion, finely chopped
  - 2 garlic cloves
  - 1 carrot, sliced into rounds
  - 2 bay leaves
  - 1 tbsp tomato paste
  - 1/2 cup olive oil
  - Salt and pepper
  - Vinegar, for serving
---

1. Rinse the lentils and put them in a pot with cold water. As soon as it comes to a boil, drain them.
2. Return them to the pot with about 1.5 liters of fresh water, the onion, garlic, carrot and bay leaves.
3. Simmer for 30-40 minutes, until the lentils are soft.
4. Add the tomato paste, olive oil, salt and pepper, and simmer for another 10 minutes until the soup thickens.
5. Serve with a splash of vinegar, olives and bread.
