---
title: Pasta with minced meat
author: Anthoula
tags: [pasta, minced meat]
translated_from: el
ingredients:
  - 1/2 kg minced meat
  - Spaghetti, any thickness you like
  - 1 medium onion
  - Dill, parsley
  - 1 cinnamon stick
  - Oil, salt, pepper
  - Tomato paste
---

1. Put the oil in the pot and let it get hot.
2. First add the onion, finely chopped in a food processor or by hand, then the dill and the parsley. Sauté them briefly, then add the minced meat.
3. Sauté everything together with the salt and pepper. Once browned, add the tomato paste, the cinnamon and enough water.
4. Let it cook in the pressure cooker for 20 minutes.
5. When it has cooled and you open the pressure cooker, if there is still water, leave it uncovered so the water cooks off and only the oil remains.
6. Boil the pasta in another pot.

Enjoy your meal!
