---
title: Spinach rice
author: Anthoula
tags: [rice, vegetables]
translated_from: el
ingredients:
  - Spinach (and other greens if you like)
  - Onion, dill, parsley
  - Rice
  - Salt, pepper
  - Oil
  - Tomato paste, if you want it red
---

1. Wash the vegetables and cut them into small pieces.
2. Put the oil in the pot and, once it is hot, add the vegetables and sauté them.
3. Then add the salt, the pepper and the tomato paste, if you want the spinach rice red. Otherwise, eat it white with lemon.
4. Once the vegetables have simmered a little, add the rice and stir constantly so it comes together with the vegetables.
5. When it is cooked, turn off the heat, let it rest a little and then serve.

Enjoy your meal!
